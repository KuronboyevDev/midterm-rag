# HiluxCare Support — Company Handbook & FAQ

This document is part of the HiluxCare knowledge base. It describes who we are,
our policies, and answers to common customer questions.

## About HiluxCare

repairs, warranty support, and genuine parts & accessories. Our goal is to keep
every Hilux on the road safely and reliably.

## Booking a service

You can book a service appointment by phone, email, or through our website's
"Book a Service" page. Please have your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) and
current mileage ready. Standard maintenance slots are available within 3–5
business days; urgent safety concerns are prioritized for same-day or next-day
appointments where possible.

## Warranty support

New Toyota Hilux vehicles purchased through HiluxCare include the manufacturer's
new-vehicle warranty. To make a warranty claim, contact support with your VIN, a
description of the issue, and the date you first noticed it. Warranty repairs
must be carried out by an authorized service center to remain valid. Wear items
(such as brake pads, wiper blades, and tires) and damage from misuse, accidents,
or unauthorized modifications are generally not covered.

## Returns and refunds (parts & accessories)

Unused parts and accessories may be returned within 30 days of purchase with the
original receipt for a full refund. Electrical parts and special-order items are
non-returnable once installed or opened, unless they are faulty. Refunds are
processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

## Roadside assistance

Eligible vehicles include 24/7 roadside assistance for the duration of the
warranty period. Services include battery jump-starts, flat-tire changes,
emergency fuel delivery, lockout assistance, and towing to the nearest
authorized service center. Call the roadside hotline printed on your membership
card or contact our support line.

## Common questions

**How often should I service my Hilux?**
Follow the maintenance schedule in your owner's manual — typically every 10,000
km or 6 months, whichever comes first. Severe-duty use (towing, off-road, dusty
conditions) may require more frequent service.

**Can I get a loaner vehicle during repairs?**
Loaner vehicles are available on request for repairs expected to take longer than
one business day, subject to availability.

**How do I escalate an unresolved issue?**
If your issue has not been resolved, ask our assistant to raise a support ticket,
or email support@hiluxcare.example with "ESCALATION" in the subject line. A
support specialist will respond within one business day.

**What information do I need to open a support ticket?**
Please provide your full name, email address, a short summary of the issue, and a
detailed description. The more detail you provide, the faster we can help.
